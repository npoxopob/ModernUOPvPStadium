Counter Explanations

Paladin » Vampire / Necromancer

Holy bonus damage (+5 to +11), Holy Essence direct damage, blessed weapon x1.5 multiplier. Chaos classes take significantly more damage from paladins.

Necromancer » Paladin (partial)

Holy Damage Reduction (25-60%) on robes, Light Bonus damage (+5 to +11) on staves. But paladin's burst is still strong — necro can mitigate but not fully counter.

Amazon » Paladin / Berserker

Paralyze locks prevent melee engagement. Poison + STR debuff cripples melee fighters. Distance critical from bows keeps Amazon safe. Berserker's spell immunity doesn't help vs weapon procs.

Vampire » Amazon

Poison resist/reflect shroud neutralizes Amazon's poison krysses. Lifesteal sustain outlasts Amazon's burst. Blood Amulet caps incoming spike damage.

Berserker » Necromancer

Weaken + Curse immunity blocks necro's debuffs. Fury paralyze immunity prevents control. HP regen masks sustain through Curse of Decay. High burst axes kill squishy necros fast.

Human — Adaptive

Crystal choice determines matchups. Thunder crystals drain necro mana. Life crystals sustain vs burst. Ice crystals slow melee. Shields reflect physical damage. Versatile but master of none.
